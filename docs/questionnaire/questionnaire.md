# IRO Community Questionnaire (Google Form)

**Google Form link:** _to be added_

The form branches by respondent type: **AAUA Students** answer Section A, **Indigenes / Residents** answer Section B, and both answer Section C. The form was built with `create_form.gs` (Google Apps Script).

## Section 1 – About you
1. Which of these best describes you? *(AAUA Student / Indigene or Resident of a host community)*. This answer decides which section comes next.
2. Age range: Below 18, 18–25, 26–35, 36–50, Above 50
3. Gender: Male, Female, Prefer not to say

## Section A – For AAUA Students
1. Your level: 100–500, Postgraduate
2. Your faculty
3. Where do you live? On-campus hostel, Off-campus in Akungba, Iwaro, Etioro, Ayegunle, or another community
4. Have you ever had an issue involving indigenes, landlords or the host community? Yes / No
5. Which issues have you experienced or witnessed? Insecurity, theft or cult activity; rent or accommodation disputes; harassment or extortion; student–indigene clashes; roads and transport; water and electricity; sanitation; none; other
6. How did you report the issue? Did not report it, SUG, landlord or caretaker, university security, police, social media, community leader, other

## Section B – For Indigenes / Residents
1. Your community: Akungba, Iwaro, Etioro, Ayegunle, other
2. Your role: landlord or caretaker, trader, transporter, community or youth leader, civil servant, farmer, resident, other
3. How long have you lived in the community?
4. Have you ever had an issue involving AAUA students? Yes / No
5. Which issues have you experienced or witnessed? Unpaid rent or property damage, noise, theft or insecurity, cult activity, clashes, land disputes, sanitation, none, other
6. How did you report the issue? Did not report it, Oba or community leader, university management, SUG, police, social media

## Section C – Reporting & the IRO platform (everyone)
1. How satisfied are you with the current ways of reporting? (1–5)
2. What stops people from reporting issues? (checkboxes)
3. Would you use a web platform (IRO) to report and track issues? Yes / Maybe / No
4. How important is anonymous reporting? (1–5)
5. Which features would you want? Anonymous reporting, tracking ID, SMS/WhatsApp updates, photo upload, Yoruba option, emergency contacts, joint response desk
6. Which device would you mostly use?
7. Any other suggestions?
