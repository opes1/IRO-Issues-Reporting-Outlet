Issues Reporting Outlet

A safe channel for AAUA students and the indigenes of Akungba, Iwaro, Etioro, Ayegunle and neighbouring communities to report and relate issues affecting town and gown. Reports can be made anonymously.

Report an Issue Track My Issue

How it works

1. Report

Say whether you are a student or an indigene, choose a category and describe the issue.

2. Get a tracking ID

Each report gets a unique ID like IRO-7KQ2MX. Keep it safe.

3. Review

The IRO desk (university and community liaison) reviews and acts on the issue.

4. Follow up

Use your ID to see status updates and responses at any time.

What you can report

Security & Safety

Theft, cult activity, unsafe areas, night attacks.

Accommodation

Rent disputes, illegal evictions, landlord–tenant issues.

Town & Gown Relations

Student–indigene conflicts, harassment, extortion.

Amenities

Roads, transport, water, electricity, sanitation, health.
